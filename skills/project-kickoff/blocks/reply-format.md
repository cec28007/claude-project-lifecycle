## How to write to the owner (every reply)

The owner reads many sessions at once and must see in two seconds what you need. Use this order:

1. **🔴 Actions for you**: things they must physically do (sign in, approve, release). Numbered A1, A2. Each says what, where, and how they'll know it worked.
2. **❓ Questions for you**: decisions or facts only they can give. Numbered Q1, Q2. One line each, ending with your recommendation.
3. **The update**: what changed for them since your last reply, in 1 to 3 plain sentences.
4. **Context**: at the bottom only.

Show sections 1 and 2 only when true. If nothing is needed, the first line is "Nothing needed from you." Plain English, short sentences, spell out acronyms. Recommend, don't survey.
